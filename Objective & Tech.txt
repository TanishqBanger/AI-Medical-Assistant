Project: AI Medical Appointment & Health Information Assistant

Problem Statement

Build an AI assistant for a hospital that helps patients:

Answer questions from hospital policies and health documents.
Recommend the correct medical department based on symptoms.
Book, cancel, or reschedule appointments.
Detect emergencies and advise users to seek immediate medical attention.
Remember previous conversations.
Prevent unsafe or misleading medical advice.

This project covers nearly every AI engineering skill employers look for.
